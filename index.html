Can you work with our IT team?

Yes—every engagement has a technical lead and change manager. We co-author acceptance criteria, run joint QA, and train your team before handoff.

How do we measure ROI?

Diagnostic sprint defines baseline KPIs (hours saved, revenue lift, error rate). Pilots ship with dashboards + weekly readouts so finance and ops can quantify the impact.
