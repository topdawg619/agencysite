Agentra Privacy Notice

Effective February 20, 2026 · Contact: privacy@agentra.ai

1. What we collect

When you visit Agentra properties, we collect only the data needed to run and secure the site:

  • Basic analytics (page views, browser type, approximate region)
  • Contact details you voluntarily submit (email, company, name)
  • Operational metadata when you authenticate with an Agentra service (timestamps, IP address, access tokens)

2. How we use the data

We process personal data to:

  • Respond to demo or support requests
  • Secure our infrastructure (audit logs, fraud detection)
  • Send product updates or operational notices (you can opt out at any time)
  • Fulfill legal obligations and enforce our agreements

3. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your information. Data may be shared with:

  • Cloud providers (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, AWS) that host Agentra apps
  • Analytics / monitoring vendors we use to maintain uptime
  • Law enforcement when legally compelled

4. Data retention & rights

Operational logs are retained for up to 12 months unless a longer period is required for audits or dispute resolution. You may:

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Ask us to correct, delete, or restrict processing
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Email privacy@agentra.ai with your request.

5. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and continuous monitoring on our GitHub, Cloudflare, and cloud accounts. Managed Sid agents are restricted to least privilege scopes.

6. Updates

This notice may change as we add new services. We will post the effective date and highlight material updates.
